# dirtree 0.0.0.9000 (development version)

* First release: `dirtree_data()`, `guide_dirtree()`, dataset `tax_lineages`.
* Axis-aware connectors (dtx-exact): labels start 1.2 em from their own
  trunk column and grow away from the axis line; the y-axis elbow runs from
  the parent's trunk across 1 em to the label; on the x-axis the tree is
  rotated - one stem drops from the (horizontal) parent trunk into each
  label corner.
